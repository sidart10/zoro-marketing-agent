# Video-script examples

These are original composites. They demonstrate instructional structure without copying a creator's
phrasing or persona.

Provenance note: the structural annotations were informed by saved transcripts across tech
productivity, AI automation, and official product tutorials. No transcript wording is reproduced.

## Informative YouTube opening — tech productivity

**Route:** YouTube script × tech productivity × explainer

**[A-ROLL]**

Most people try to fix an AI assistant's memory by giving it more context.

That works until the context becomes the problem: old instructions conflict, important decisions are
buried, and every new task starts by rereading a small library.

In this video, I'll show you a simpler model. Keep three things separate: the rules that rarely
change, the working memory that changes often, and the source material loaded only when a task needs
it.

**[B-ROLL: three labeled layers appear—Rules, Working Memory, On-Demand Sources]**

We'll build each layer, test it on a real research task, and finish with the warning signs that tell
you the system is becoming bloated again.

**Why it works:** the hook names a failure, the visual supplies a mental model, and the promise tells
viewers what they will be able to do—not merely what topics will be discussed.

## Short-form script — AI automation

**Route:** short-form video × AI automation × tutorial

**[A-ROLL]**

Before you automate a task with AI, write down these five words.

**[ON SCREEN: Trigger · Input · Decision · Review · Failure]**

What starts the workflow? Which input is trusted? What judgment can AI make? Who reviews a risky
action? And where does the work go when something breaks?

If you cannot answer all five, the process is not ready for automation. You are about to make
ambiguity run faster.

Document the workflow first. Then choose the tool.

**Why it works:** the hook creates an immediate action, the on-screen device makes the framework
memorable, and the close resolves the promise without a generic follow request.
